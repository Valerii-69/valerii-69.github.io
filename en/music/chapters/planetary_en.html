Planetary Rhythms

Literary Essay

Each planet moves along its orbit, creating rhythms that influence each other. Music here reflects cosmic cycles: slow turns, fast rotations, and harmonic ratios. Every sound echoes the celestial bodies.

Mathematical Model

We use circular orbits with different radii and speeds:
x = cx + R*cos(ω*t + φ)
y = cy + R*sin(ω*t + φ)
where R is orbit radius, ω is angular speed, φ is initial phase. Canvas visualizes planets and their rhythms.
