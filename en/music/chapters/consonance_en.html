Story 10: Mathematics of Consonance

Literary version:

Sounds can be not only beautiful but mathematically ordered. When notes form harmonies, their relationships—fractions and proportions—create consonance.

Mathematical model:

Harmonic sequence uses ratios f2/f1 = p/q, where p and q are integers. Each ratio produces a consonance audible in the musical line.
