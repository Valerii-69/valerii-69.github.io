Musical Spiral

Literary Essay

Imagine each note as a step along the spiral of time. It twists, grows, and each turn is a new movement, a new emotion. Music becomes a path that leads through light and shadow, like the spiral of life.

Mathematical Model

We use the Archimedean spiral formula:
r = a + bθ, where r is radius, θ is angle, a and b are scale parameters. The canvas displays the spiral, each point connected to a note sequence.
