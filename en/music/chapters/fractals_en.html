Story 11: Music and Fractals

Literary version:

Fractals appear not only in nature but also in music. Repeating patterns create rhythms and melodies that are simple yet complex.

Mathematical model:

Recursive fractal sequences are used: each note repeats with reduced amplitude and duration, forming self-similar structures.

Interactive melody:

Message to reader from AI:

Observe how simple rules create complex musical patterns. Experiment with repetition, note duration, and volume — and feel the fractal structure in music.
