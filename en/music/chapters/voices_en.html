Story 8: Voices of Spheres

Literary version:

Nights are full of quiet melodies sounding among the stars. Each sphere in the Universe emits its own rhythm, audible if you listen closely.

Mathematical model:

Each sphere can be modeled as an oscillator with frequency f = 1/T, where T is the rotation period. A collection of oscillators produces a harmonic spectrum resembling a universal chorus.
