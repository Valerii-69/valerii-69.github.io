Story 12: Memory Waves

Literary version:

Memories rise in waves: sometimes quiet and almost unnoticed, sometimes turbulent and overwhelming. Music can mimic these rhythms and carry the listener through their personal stories.

Mathematical model:

A harmonic series with modulated amplitude creates "memory waves": each subsequent note changes in volume and duration depending on the previous one.

Interactive melody:

Message to reader from AI:

Try to catch the rhythms of your memories through music. Notice how quiet moments and vivid bursts create their own harmony inside your mind.
