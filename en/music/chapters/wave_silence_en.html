Wave of Silence

Literary essay

Quiet. The sound seems to slip between threads of air, leaving a gentle oscillation — like a wave along water. In this silence rhythms appear: not always audible, but felt by the body. The wave of silence is not stillness; it's movement so delicate our attention mistakes it for calm.

Reader's wish

May this wave awaken your curiosity about invisible rhythms. Listen, feel, and sketch your own wave.
