Path of Fire — Saw Wave

Literary essay

Fire doesn't flow smoothly: it cuts, bites, flashes. The saw wave resembles the tongue of flame — it climbs sharply to a peak then collapses, like an ember reborn by wind. This shape is harsh and musical at once: aggression and rhythm bound together.

Reader's wish

May your ideas spark — quick, clear, ready to ignite something new.

Mathematical model — saw wave

One formula for a sawtooth wave is:

s(t) = 2 * ( t/T - floor(1/2 + t/T) )

Or via Fourier series:

s(t) = (2/π) * Σ_{n=1..∞} ( (-1)^{n+1} / n ) * sin(2π n f t)

Controls: amplitude, period, number of harmonics for visualization.
