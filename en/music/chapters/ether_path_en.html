Path of Ether — Harmonic Shadows

Literary essay

Ether is not emptiness but a fine fabric where harmonics tremble. Shadows here are not lack of light but interference patterns — delicate overlays of waves that create new shades of sound. In harmonic shadows we hear past and sense future.

Reader's wish

May these shadows hint at new chords — listen closely and they will reveal unexpected melodies.

Mathematical model — harmonic series & interference

Any periodic motion can be expanded in a Fourier series — sum of sinusoids with frequencies n·f₀. Resonance and shadows occur when frequencies match or are close.

y(x,t) = Σ_{n=1..N} A_n · sin(2π n f₀ t + φ_n)
Interference: y_total = y1 + y2 → constructive/destructive depending on phase.
