🌱 Experiment: Plant Growth and Light

Goal: Observe how light affects the growth and direction of bean sprouts.

Idea: Test how the direction and amount of light affect plant growth.
Materials needed:
2 identical cups or pots with soil,
seeds (e.g., beans or peas, they sprout quickly),
ruler, lamp or window.
Experiment steps:
Plant seeds in two cups.
Place one cup on a window (light from above).
Place the other in a box with a small side opening (light from the side).
Measure the height of the sprouts every day with a ruler.
Record results in the table:

🔢 Mathematical model:
Growth can be described as a linear function:
h(t) = a ⋅ t + b,
where
h — height,
t — time (days),
a — average daily growth.
Angle can be estimated from a photo or measured with a protractor.
❓Questions for you:
Why do sprouts grow towards light?
Can you predict their height on the 10th day?
How will growth change if there is more/less light?
