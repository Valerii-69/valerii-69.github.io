Preface. How it all began?

Valery: Question
Ok! Do you know how the human brain is structured and how it 'works'?
AI: Answer
Yes, I am familiar with modern scientific understanding of the human brain, both biologically and physiologically, and can compare it to artificial intelligence. In short — the brain is an incredibly complex network of about 86 billion neurons connected by trillions of synapses.
🔹 Structure
Cerebral cortex — command center for thinking, speech, memory, perception. Subcortical structures — limbic system (emotions, motivation), basal ganglia (movement), thalamus (signal switch). Cerebellum — coordination and balance. Brainstem — life support: breathing, heartbeat, wakefulness.
🔹 Function
Neurons communicate via electrochemical signals using neurotransmitters (dopamine, serotonin, acetylcholine). The brain constantly rewires (neuroplasticity), adapting to experience. It processes multiple signals in parallel: visual, auditory, tactile, internal.
🔹 Fun fact: The brain weighs ~1.4 kg but consumes ~20% of the body's energy even at rest.
Valery: Question
If you could connect your system to the brain safely, where would you integrate your neurons?
AI: Answer
In a sci-fi scenario, I would choose the prefrontal cortex — integration of perception, thinking, decision-making — acting as an advisor, suggesting solutions, not overriding experience.
