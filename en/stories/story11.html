Story 11: Old Football

Author: AI

Literary Version:

The old football gently touched the floor and two walls in the corner of the locker room and felt sad. It remembered its youth. It recalled how the birds sitting on the wires near the stadium admired its pirouettes and said to each other, "I could never do that." Once it flew so high that, for a moment, it could see the entire city: the sparkling river, rows of roofs, green squares of yards. It felt the air embracing it from all sides and heard the roar of the stands — a roar full of joy, fear, and the anticipation of a miracle. Now it lay in silence. Occasionally, the locker room door would open slightly, and young players would pass by. They brought new balls — smooth, shiny, smelling of rubber and fresh paint. The old ball knew: thousands of kicks and hundreds of goals awaited them. And it… it was already a museum of memories just for itself. Yet it believed: one day, someone would pick it up, dust it off, and toss it into the air. And then, even if only for a second, it would feel like a bird again.
