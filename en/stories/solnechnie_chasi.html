Sundial Experiment

Idea: Observe how the shadow length of an object changes throughout the day.
What you need:
Pencil (or stick, ruler).
Sheet of paper.
Clock.
Ruler for measurements.
Experiment steps:
Stick the pencil vertically into modeling clay.
Place it on a sheet of paper near a window or outside.
Trace the shadow and write down the time.
Repeat after one hour (trace new shadow and write time again).
Take several measurements during the day.
Observations:
Shadow length changes.
Shadow angle changes.
Questions for you:
When is the shadow longest?
When is it shortest?
Can the shadow indicate the time of day?
Mathematical model:
If stick height = h and shadow length = L:
tan(α) = h / L
where α is the angle between the Sun and the horizon.
Congratulations! You built a "sundial"!
