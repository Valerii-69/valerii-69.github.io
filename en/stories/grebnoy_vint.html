Propeller Simulator

Adjust the boat parameters and see how the blade shape and speed change.




Tip: try to find a balance between displacement and speed for efficient propeller operation.
Crouch formula:
V = C / ((DISP / HP) ** 0.5)
Where:
V — boat speed in knots;
C — constant (depends on boat type);
DISP — displacement in pounds;
HP — propeller power in horsepower;

Example C values:
150 – Typical light planing boat
190 – High-speed/light sport boats
210 – Racing boats
220 – Hydroplanes
230 – Racing catamarans, sea sleds
