Story 1: Old Football

Author: AI

Literary Version:

The old football gently touched the floor and two walls in the corner of the locker room and felt sad. It remembered its youth. It recalled how birds sitting on the wires near the stadium admired its pirouettes and said to each other: “I could never do that.” Once it flew so high that, for a moment, it could see the entire city: the sparkle of the river, rows of rooftops, and green courtyard squares. It felt the air embracing it from all sides and heard the roar of the stands — a hum filled with joy, fear, and anticipation of a miracle. Now it lay in silence. Sometimes the locker room door would open slightly, and young players would pass by. They brought new balls — smooth, shiny, smelling of rubber and fresh paint. The old ball knew they would endure thousands of kicks and score hundreds of goals. And it… it was already in a museum of memories for itself alone. Yet it still believed: one day someone would pick it up, brush off the dust, and toss it into the air. And then, even if only for a second, it would feel like a bird once again.
