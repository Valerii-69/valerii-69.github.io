Story 6: Origami and Bridge

Author: Valerii

I watched a person using the art of origami to fold a small paper dinosaur.

AI Version "Origami and Bridge"

In the evening, walking through a small park, I stopped by a bench where an elderly man was folding a small dinosaur out of paper. His movements were slow, precise, and almost ceremonial — each fold like a line in a poem, each turn of paper like the breath of an ancient creature. He was a master of origami. He noticed my interest and, smiling, handed me another sheet of paper: — Try it yourself. It’s not just a toy — it’s art and… mathematics. Thus began my encounter with the geometry of paper worlds.
