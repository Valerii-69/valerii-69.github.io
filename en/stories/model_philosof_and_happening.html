Model — Philosophy and Chance

In this section, we explore a simple mathematical model of random choice that illustrates how probabilities affect outcomes.

$$ P(A) = \frac{n(A)}{n(S)} $$

Model Description:

Imagine a basket with colored balls. Each color represents an event. The probability of picking a ball of a certain color is proportional to the number of balls of that color.

Questions to Consider:
